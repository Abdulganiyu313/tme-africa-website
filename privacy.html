Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what information Trans-Africa Machinery and Engineering Ltd. ("TME Africa," "we," "us") collects through tme-africa.com, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It applies to this website only.

Who we are

TME Africa is an industrial equipment supplier registered in Nigeria (RC 9662149), operating from Sagamu, Ogun State. You can reach us at info@tme-africa.com or +234 811 778 8403.

Information we collect

We collect information directly from you when you use a form on this site — the general enquiry form, the Workshop Performance Audit booking form, or the Mechanical Fitting job request form. Depending on which form you use, this may include:

  • Your name and the name of your company
  • Phone number and, if you provide it, email address
  • Your industry, location, and a description of your equipment needs or the job you want done
  • A photo or video of equipment or a job site, if you choose to attach one
  • Any other detail you choose to type into a message field

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information through this site — no pricing is quoted online, and no purchases are made here.

Automatically collected information

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are viewed, how people arrive here, and which buttons and links are clicked (including WhatsApp and phone-call clicks, and form submissions). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and used to improve the site; we do not use it to identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using browser extensions such as Google's own Analytics Opt-out Add-on, or by using your browser's "Do Not Track" / cookie-blocking settings.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, quote request, audit booking, or job request
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about the request you submitted
  • To keep records of enquiries for our own business purposes
  • To understand and improve how this website is used

We do not use your information for marketing you did not ask for, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party.

Who we share it with

Form submissions on this site are processed using EmailJS, a third-party service that delivers your submission to our email inbox and, if you provide an email address, sends you an automatic confirmation. EmailJS processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy, available at emailjs.com. We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by Nigerian law, or with your explicit consent (for example, if you agree to be named as a client reference).

If you contact us via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's (Meta's) own privacy policy and terms, which we do not control.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, follow up on a potential project, and maintain business records — typically no longer than a few years unless a longer period is required for legal, accounting, or contractual reasons.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to ask us what information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, and to ask us to delete it, subject to any legal obligation we may have to keep it. To make any of these requests, email info@tme-africa.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adults sourcing industrial equipment and services. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site or our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information: info@tme-africa.com or +234 811 778 8403.
