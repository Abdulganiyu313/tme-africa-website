Heavy-Duty Workshop Machinery Installation

Commissioning — Kaduna State

SectorMetal Fabrication & Engineering
LocationKaduna State, Nigeria
EquipmentLathe, Milling & Hydraulic Press Line
Units5 Machine Installations
Timeline10 Weeks Factory to Commission
Year2024
The Challenge

The client, a growing fabrication workshop, needed to upgrade from manual hand tools to a fully mechanized production floor capable of precision turning, milling, and heavy-duty metal forming — without halting existing operations during the transition.

Our Solution

Trans-Africa Machinery and Engineering Ltd. sourced five core machines direct from our OEM partner factory — a heavy-duty lathe, universal milling machine, and 200-ton hydraulic press — customised to 415V site power. Our engineering team phased the installation around the client's existing production schedule, completed full electrical commissioning, and trained 8 workshop technicians.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does TME Africa provide?

Full-spectrum industrial integration — sourcing and procurement, customs clearance, installation and commissioning, lifecycle maintenance, spare parts, and on-site technician training, all through one partner.

Do you handle import duties and customs clearance?

Yes. Every shipment includes full Nigerian customs and import documentation handled by our team, so equipment arrives ready for installation without added paperwork on your end.

What after-sales support do you offer?

We offer 6- and 12-month preventative maintenance contracts, spare parts procurement direct from our OEM partners, and monthly on-site lubrication, tuning, and inspection visits to keep equipment running at spec.
