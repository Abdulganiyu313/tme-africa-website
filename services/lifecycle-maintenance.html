Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need a contract, or can you do one-off repairs?

Both are available — a retainer SLA for ongoing cover, or a single call-out for a specific breakdown.

What’s covered under a 6 vs 12-month SLA?

Scope and visit frequency are agreed upfront per machine, based on your production schedule.

Do you stock spare parts?

Spares are sourced direct from OEM partners, with common wear parts held in stock for faster turnaround.

Is this the same as the free Workshop Performance Audit?

No. The Workshop Performance Audit is a free, one-time assessment. This is an ongoing maintenance relationship, usually the first thing an audit recommends.
