Full Coverage

The 7 Areas We Audit

1. Machines & Equipment

Condition, accuracy, age, real utilisation per machine, idle assets, machines wrong for the job.

2. Maintenance Practice

Servicing schedule and whether it’s followed, records, lubrication, daily operator checks, who does repairs.

3. Spare Parts & Tooling

Critical spares held on site, tooling condition, replacement lead times, emergency buying.

4. Downtime & Bottlenecks

What breaks most often, repair turnaround, cost per hour of downtime, jobs delayed or refused.

5. Power & Utilities

Supply stability, generator and stabiliser sizing, earthing and panels, compressed air and coolant.

6. Safety & Layout

Guarding, emergency stops, PPE, housekeeping, workshop flow, lighting and ventilation.

7. People & Skills

Operator training levels, single points of failure, appetite for training.

The Deliverables

What You Receive

  • A written report rating each of the seven areas: good / fair / poor.
  • The top 3 issues and what each is costing you.
  • A priority action list: fix now, within 30 days, within 90 days.
  • A recommended spare parts list for your critical machines.
  • A maintenance schedule with daily, weekly and monthly checks your team can follow.
