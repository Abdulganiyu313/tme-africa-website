Full Capability

What We Do

1. Machine Installation & Setting

Positioning, levelling, anchoring and baseplate work, so the machine sits true from day one.

2. Shaft & Coupling Alignment

Laser and dial-gauge alignment of motors, pumps, gearboxes and drive lines to reduce vibration and bearing failure.

3. Bearing & Seal Replacement

Removal, fitting and pre-loading of bearings, bushes and mechanical seals.

4. Power Transmission

Belts, chains, sprockets, pulleys, couplings and gearbox fitting, tensioning and adjustment.

5. Pump, Compressor & Conveyor Work

Strip-down, overhaul, reassembly and recommissioning.

6. Pipework & Structural Fitting

Fabrication support, pipe fitting, guards, frames and platforms.

7. Dismantling & Relocation

Moving a machine or a whole workshop, including rigging, transport support and re-commissioning at the new site.

8. Breakdown Response

Emergency fitting work to get a critical machine back into production.
